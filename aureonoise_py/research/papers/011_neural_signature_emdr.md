# 011 — Neural Signature of EMDR Stimulation

## Metadata
- **Title:** Facilitating Access to Emotions: Neural Signature of EMDR Stimulation
- **Authors:** Deborah Herkt, Visal Tumani, Georg Gron, Thomas Kammer, Arne Hofmann, Birgit Abler
- **Year:** 2014
- **Journal:** PLoS One
- **DOI:** 10.1371/journal.pone.0106350
- **PMC:** PMC4148424
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4148424/

## Abstract
Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing (EMDR) is a method in psychotherapy effective in treating symptoms of posttraumatic stress disorder. The client attends to alternating bilateral visual, auditory or sensory stimulation while confronted with emotionally disturbing material. It is thought that the bilateral stimulation as a specific element of EMDR facilitates accessing and processing of negative material while presumably creating new associative links. We hypothesized that the putatively facilitated access should be reflected in increased activation of the amygdala upon bilateral EMDR stimulation even in healthy subjects. We investigated 22 healthy female university students with fMRI. Subjects were scanned while confronted with blocks of disgusting and neutral picture stimuli. One third of the blocks was presented without any additional stimulation, one third with bilateral simultaneous auditory stimulation, and one third with bilateral alternating auditory stimulation as used in EMDR. Contrasting disgusting vs. neutral picture stimuli confirmed the expected robust effect of amygdala activation for all auditory stimulation conditions. The interaction analysis with the type of auditory stimulation revealed a specific increase in activation of the right amygdala for the bilateral alternating auditory stimulation. Activation of the left dorsolateral prefrontal cortex showed the opposite effect with decreased activation.

## Key Findings
- Bilateral ALTERNATING auditory stimulation specifically increased right amygdala activation
- Bilateral SIMULTANEOUS stimulation did not produce this effect — alternation is key
- Left dorsolateral prefrontal cortex showed decreased activation (reduced cognitive control)
- The pattern (limbic up + frontal down) facilitates emotional processing and reintegration

## Relevance to Aureonoise
Critical distinction: it is the ALTERNATING nature of bilateral stimulation that produces the neural signature, not just bilateral presentation. Simultaneous bilateral audio does not produce EMDR-like effects. This directly validates Aureonoise's alternating bilateral panning mechanism over simple stereo presentation. The decreased frontal control + increased amygdala access pattern is the therapeutic mechanism for trauma processing.
