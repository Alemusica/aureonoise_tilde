## Abstract

The distribution of EEG oscillation periods in healthy volunteers is symmetrical between right and left hemispheres. Patients with functional brain disorders (asthenoneurotic syndrome) show asymmetrical structure of EEG oscillation period distribution. A course of acoustic EEG-controlled biological feedback (bioacoustic correction, BAC) decreases the asymmetry to a level characteristic of healthy individuals.

The BAC method: 4-channel EEG (Fp1, Fp2, O1, O2 — frontal and occipital, both hemispheres) is recorded and transformed in real time into acoustic signals via a proprietary algorithm. All 4 channels are transformed simultaneously but independently. Stereo presentation: left hemisphere signals to left ear, right hemisphere to right ear. The patient hears a "polyphonic, music-like" sonification of their own brain activity.

## Key Findings
- **Healthy brain = symmetric EEG** between hemispheres
- **Pathological brain = asymmetric EEG** between hemispheres
- BAC restores hemispheric symmetry by feeding back sonified EEG
- The transformation preserves ALL frequency components simultaneously (delta through beta) — does NOT isolate narrow bands
- Protocol: 20-30 minute sessions, 10-15 procedures, 2-3x per week
- 85% positive results across ASD, depression, anxiety, chronic fatigue, focal brain lesions
- Device: Synchro-S (SinKor LLC, St. Petersburg)
