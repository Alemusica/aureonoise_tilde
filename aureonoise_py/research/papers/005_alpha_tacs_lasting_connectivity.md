# 005 — Alpha tACS Lasting Connectivity and Anxiety Reduction

## Metadata
- **Title:** Lasting connectivity increase and anxiety reduction via transcranial alternating current stimulation
- **Authors:** Kevin J Clancy, Sarah K Baisley, Alejandro Albizu, Nika Kartvelishvili, Mingzhou Ding, Wen Li
- **Year:** 2018
- **Journal:** Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience
- **DOI:** 10.1093/scan/nsy096
- **PMC:** PMC6277743
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6277743/

## Abstract
Growing evidence of transcranial alternating current stimulation (tACS) modulating intrinsic neural oscillations has spawned interest in applying tACS to treat psychiatric disorders associated with aberrant neural oscillations. The alpha rhythmic activity is known to dominate neural oscillations at the awake, restful state, while attenuated resting-state alpha activity has been implicated in anxious mood. Administering repeated alpha-frequency tACS (at individual peak alpha frequency; 8-12 Hz) over four consecutive days (in the experiment group, sham stimulation in the control group), we demonstrated immediate and lasting (>24 h) increases in resting-state posterior-frontal connectivity in the alpha frequency, quantified by Granger causality. Critically, this connectivity enhancement was accompanied by sustained reductions in both anxious arousal and negative perception of sensory stimuli. Resting-state alpha power also increased, albeit only transiently, reversing to the baseline level within 24 h after tACS. Therefore, the lasting enhancement of long-range alpha connectivity due to alpha-tACS differs from local alpha activity that is nonetheless conserved, highlighting the adaptability of alpha oscillatory networks.

## Key Findings
- Multi-session alpha-tACS produced lasting (>24h) posterior-frontal alpha connectivity increases
- Anxious arousal decreased both immediately and throughout the 4-day study
- Local alpha power increased only transiently; connectivity changes persisted
- Connectivity enhancement (not local power) mediated behavioral improvements

## Relevance to Aureonoise
Critical finding: it is connectivity enhancement, not raw alpha power, that produces lasting therapeutic effects. Aureonoise should target alpha-frequency coherence between brain regions (via bilateral presentation) rather than simply boosting alpha amplitude. The multi-session protocol (4 consecutive days) informs recommended usage patterns. The anxiety reduction validates one of Aureonoise's primary therapeutic targets.
