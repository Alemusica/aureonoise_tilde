## Key Findings (from related literature context)
- Bioacoustic correction involves real-time conversion of EEG signals to auditory stimuli
- The method promotes hemispheric symmetry by feeding back brain's own electrical activity as sound
- BAC has shown effects on interhemispheric coherence and EEG normalization
- The approach is related to neurofeedback but uses acoustic conversion rather than visual display

## Relevance to Aureonoise
BAC represents an alternative approach to Aureonoise: instead of imposing external therapeutic frequencies, it converts the brain's own activity to sound and feeds it back. While Aureonoise takes the opposite approach (external stimulation driving brain activity), the BAC concept informs a potential future mode: adaptive stimulation that responds to the user's brain state. The hemispheric symmetry finding validates Aureonoise's bilateral mechanism for promoting interhemispheric balance.
