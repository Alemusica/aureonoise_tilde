## Key Findings (from related literature, see paper 038 for EEG data)
- Shamanic drumming typically occurs at 3-4.5 Hz (theta range)
- Prolonged rhythmic drumming shifts EEG towards theta dominance
- The monotonous repetitive nature induces auditory driving effects
- Drumming-induced states correlate with increased theta coherence
- The combination of motor and auditory rhythmicity enhances entrainment

## Relevance to Aureonoise
The shamanic drumming tempo (3-4.5 Hz) falls within the theta band, linking ancient healing practices to modern neuroscience. The auditory driving effect (neural entrainment to rhythmic auditory input) is the same mechanism Aureonoise exploits for all its frequency modes.
