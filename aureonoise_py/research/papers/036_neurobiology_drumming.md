## Key Findings (from literature context and related research)
- Rhythmic drumming induces altered states of consciousness through neural entrainment
- Drumming at specific tempos (4-7 Hz, theta range) promotes meditative/trance states
- The repetitive percussive nature provides discrete onset events ideal for neural phase-locking
- Drumming activates motor, auditory, and limbic networks simultaneously
- Cross-cultural universality of drumming in healing rituals suggests deep neurobiological basis
