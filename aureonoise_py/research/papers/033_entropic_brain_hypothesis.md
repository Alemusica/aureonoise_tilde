# 033 — The Entropic Brain Hypothesis

## Metadata
- **Title:** The entropic brain: a theory of conscious states informed by neuroimaging research with psychedelic drugs
- **Authors:** Robin L Carhart-Harris, Robert Leech, Peter J Hellyer, Murray Shanahan, Amanda Feilding, Enzo Tagliazucchi, Dante R Chialvo, David Nutt
- **Year:** 2014
- **Journal:** Frontiers in Human Neuroscience
- **DOI:** 10.3389/fnhum.2014.00020
- **PMC:** PMC3909994
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3909994/

## Abstract
Entropy measures uncertainty about system states and can indicate physical disorder. This work applies entropy to consciousness states, emphasizing the psychedelic state as exemplifying a primitive consciousness form. Based on psilocybin neuroimaging, the study argues that primary states display elevated entropy in brain functional connectivity patterns. The psychedelic state exhibits greater connectivity motifs than normal waking consciousness, suggesting criticality. Normal waking consciousness exhibits entropy suppression, operating just below criticality, providing constrained consciousness with metacognitive functions including reality-testing and self-awareness. Entry into primary states involves default-mode network collapse and decoupling from medial temporal lobes.

## Key Findings
- Psilocybin decreases cerebral blood flow and oscillatory power in high-level association cortices
- DMN functional connectivity significantly decreases under psilocybin
- Network metastability and entropy increase in association networks post-drug
- Decreased PCC alpha power correlates with reported ego-disintegration
- Greater repertoire of connectivity motifs during psychedelic vs baseline states
- Normal consciousness operates just below criticality (entropy suppression)

## Relevance to Aureonoise
The entropic brain theory positions consciousness on a spectrum from low entropy (rigid, constrained — as in depression/addiction) to high entropy (flexible, unconstrained — as in psychedelic states). Aureonoise's modes should map to this spectrum: calming/alpha modes reduce entropy (stabilize DMN), while gamma/complex modes increase entropy (promote flexibility). The finding that PCC alpha power correlates with ego-disintegration directly links Aureonoise's alpha manipulation to consciousness state modulation.
