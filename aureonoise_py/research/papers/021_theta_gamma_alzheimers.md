## Key Findings (from literature context)
- Theta-gamma cross-frequency coupling is disrupted in Alzheimer's disease
- The disruption correlates with cognitive decline and memory impairment
- Restoring theta-gamma coupling is a potential therapeutic target
- Entrainment at these frequencies may compensate for endogenous coupling loss
