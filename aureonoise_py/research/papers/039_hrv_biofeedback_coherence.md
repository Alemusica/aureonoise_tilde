## Abstract
This research examined 1.8 million user sessions from a mobile app (2019-2020) studying HRV coherence and emotional stability. The analysis found that positive emotions reported by users were associated with higher Coherence scores and more stable HRV frequencies, while negative emotions showed lower scores and dispersed frequency distributions. The most prevalent coherence frequency identified was 0.10 Hz, though many high-coherence users operated between 0.04-0.10 Hz. Users demonstrated frequency stability across sessions, increasing with higher coherence levels.

## Key Findings
- Positive emotions associated with higher HRV Coherence scores and more stable frequencies
- Most prevalent coherence frequency: 0.10 Hz (6 breaths/minute)
- Many high-coherence users operate between 0.04-0.10 Hz
- Individuals naturally discover their unique resonant frequency through slower, deeper breathing
- Frequency stability across sessions increases with coherence level
