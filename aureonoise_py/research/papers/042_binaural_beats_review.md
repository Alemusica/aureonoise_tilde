# 042 — Binaural Beats: Systematic Review of Brain Oscillatory Effects

## Metadata
- **Title:** Binaural beats to entrain the brain? A systematic review of the effects of binaural beat stimulation on brain oscillatory activity, and the implications for psychological research and intervention
- **Authors:** Ruth Maria Ingendoh, Ella S Posny, Angela Heine
- **Year:** 2023
- **Journal:** PLoS One
- **DOI:** 10.1371/journal.pone.0286023
- **PMC:** PMC10198548
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10198548/

## Abstract
Binaural beats are an auditory phenomenon occurring when two tones of different frequencies presented separately to each ear create the sensation of a third tone at the difference frequency. The brainwave entrainment hypothesis proposes that external stimulation at specific frequencies causes electrocortical activity to oscillate at matching frequencies. This systematic review examined 14 published studies investigating whether binaural beat stimulation produces changes in EEG parameters. Results were inconsistent: five studies supported the entrainment hypothesis, eight reported contradictory findings, and one showed mixed results.

## Key Findings
- Inconsistent empirical evidence: 5 studies support, 8 contradict, 1 mixed
- Entrainment effects appeared in theta, alpha, and gamma but not beta
- Methodological heterogeneity limits cross-study comparability
- The brainwave entrainment hypothesis is NOT reliably supported by evidence
- Binaural beats may have effects through mechanisms other than entrainment

## Relevance to Aureonoise
This review confirms that binaural beats alone are an unreliable entrainment mechanism. 8 of 14 studies contradict the entrainment hypothesis. Aureonoise's design must not rely on binaural beats as its primary mechanism. Instead, Aureonoise uses multiple concurrent mechanisms: bilateral alternation (EMDR pathway), stochastic resonance (noise), cross-frequency coupling (phi ratios), and amplitude modulation — any one of which has stronger evidence than binaural beats alone.
