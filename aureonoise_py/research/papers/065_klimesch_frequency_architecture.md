## Abstract

Research on brain oscillations has brought up a picture of coupled oscillators. The paper analyzes how many frequencies exist, what the coupling principles are, what their functional meaning is, and whether body oscillations follow similar coupling principles. Two basic coupling principles govern brain and body oscillations: (i) amplitude (envelope) modulation between frequencies m and n, where the phase of the slower frequency m modulates the envelope of the faster frequency n, and (ii) phase coupling between m and n, where the frequency of n is a harmonic multiple of m. This leads to the binary hierarchy brain body oscillation theory. Its central hypotheses are that body oscillation frequencies can be predicted from brain oscillation frequencies and that brain and body oscillations are aligned to each other.

## Key Findings
- Brain and body oscillations form a **unified frequency system**
- Two coupling principles: **phase-amplitude** (cross-frequency) and **phase-phase** (harmonic)
- Resting state frequencies follow **golden ratio (phi) spacing** — maximally desynchronized
- Active state frequencies shift toward **harmonic (integer) ratios** — facilitating cross-frequency coupling
- The transition from phi-ratio to harmonic ratio spacing reflects the transition from rest to cognitive engagement
- Body oscillations (heart rate, respiration, gastric rhythm) are predicted by and coupled to brain oscillation frequencies
