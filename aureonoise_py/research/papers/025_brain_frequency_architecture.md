# 025 — Brain Frequency Architecture

## Metadata
- **Title:** The frequency architecture of brain and brain body oscillations: an analysis
- **Authors:** Wolfgang Klimesch
- **Year:** 2018
- **Journal:** European Journal of Neuroscience
- **DOI:** 10.1111/ejn.14192
- **PMC:** PMC6668003
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6668003/

## Abstract
Research on brain oscillations demonstrates coupled oscillators operating across multiple frequencies. The analysis addresses how many frequencies exist, their coupling mechanisms, functional significance, and whether body oscillations follow comparable principles. Two fundamental coupling mechanisms are identified: amplitude modulation (where slower frequency phase modulates faster frequency envelope) and phase coupling (where faster frequency is a harmonic multiple of slower frequency). Center frequencies suggest a binary hierarchy where neighboring frequencies maintain 1:2 ratios. The binary hierarchy brain body oscillation theory proposes that body oscillation frequencies can be predicted from brain oscillations, with alignment between brain and body systems.

## Key Findings
- Brain oscillations exhibit distinct frequency domains with preferred center frequencies
- Neighboring frequency domains maintain binary 1:2 relationships enabling optimal phase coupling
- Individual alpha frequency correlates with individual heart rate via predicted scaling factor
- Breathing frequencies align with predicted frequency hierarchy
- Brain and many body oscillations can be described by a single system governed by m:n coupling

## Relevance to Aureonoise
Klimesch's binary hierarchy model provides the alternative framework to Kramer's golden ratio model. The brain uses both: 1:2 harmonic relationships for phase coupling and phi-ratio relationships for desynchronization. Aureonoise should implement both: harmonic relationships (for entrainment modes) and phi-ratio relationships (for integration/complexity modes). The brain-body coupling finding is critical — Aureonoise's frequencies should respect the heart rate and breathing frequency relationships (HRV coherence at ~0.1 Hz connects to alpha via this hierarchy).
